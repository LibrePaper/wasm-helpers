Abstract

We study the estimation of marginal effects when the number of covariates grows with the sample size. A simple penalised estimator is shown to be consistent, and a simulation confirms the result in finite samples.

Keywords: marginal effects, sparsity, inference

2.1 Theory

Definition 1 (Sparsity). A vector is sparse if few of its entries are nonzero.

Theorem 2 (Consistency). Under sparsity, the estimator converges in probability to the truth.

Proof. Apply the law of large numbers to each nonzero coordinate and note that the remaining coordinates vanish.
